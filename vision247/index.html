Case study · 2021

OneHubTV

Reframing an OTT subscription experience around value, trust, and faster time to watching.

RoleSenior UI/UX Designer
ScopeSubscription UX · conversion
TeamProduct · marketing · engineering
SurfaceStreaming service
The opportunity

Subscription products have to earn trust before they ask for commitment.

The experience needed clearer value communication, plan comparison, and a more confident route from discovery to watching.

The design challenge was to make a complex system feel understandable at the moment a real decision had to be made.
Double Diamond · Discover

Start with the work, not the interface.

I focused on where people hesitated: unclear plans, weak value signals, and too much friction between interest and first use.

Context

Value needs to arrive before the paywall.

Story, content, and plan clarity reduce uncertainty.

Research lens

A faster path can still feel considered.

Observation, stakeholder alignment, and prototype feedback kept the work grounded in real constraints.

Double Diamond · Define

Turn complexity into a usable point of view.

We framed the experience around value upfront, plan confidence, and a shorter path to first watch.

01 · Discover

Understand people, constraints, hardware, data, and context.

02 · Define

Make the problem and decision model legible to the team.

03 · Develop

04 · Deliver

Double Diamond · Develop

Make the decision easy to understand.

The redesign clarifies content, plans, and trial expectations without sacrificing the richness of the service.

Clearer hierarchy lets the product explain its value before asking for payment.

Double Diamond · Deliver

A reusable subscription story.

The new structure supports future campaigns, content launches, and market expansion.

What changed

Less friction between interest and action.

The product makes the next step clearer at every stage.

What scales

Patterns over one-off screens.

Reusable interaction and content structures create leverage across the wider product ecosystem.
