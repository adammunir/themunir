Toro Oasis

Making pro-grade smart irrigation understandable for homeowners and contractors.

RoleSenior Designer
ScopeConsumer IoT · onboarding
TeamProduct · engineering · research
SurfaceConnected hardware ecosystem
The opportunity

Connected products are only useful when ownership feels simple.

Oasis brings hardware, setup, schedules, and ongoing care together. The design challenge was to make a technically capable system legible to first-time owners while preserving the speed contractors need.

The design challenge was to make a complex system feel understandable at the moment a real decision had to be made.
Double Diamond · Discover

Start with the work, not the interface.

I mapped the ownership journey from install and pairing through daily control, alerts, and handoff. The important moments were less about individual screens and more about confidence across the whole lifecycle.

Research lens
