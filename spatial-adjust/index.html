Spatial Adjust

A map-based recommendation system for reviewing and tuning irrigation zone changes.

RoleSenior Designer
ScopeSpatial UX · decision support
TeamProduct · engineering · domain experts
SurfaceMap and recommendation workflow
The opportunity

A recommendation is only useful when the spatial consequence is obvious.

Spatial Adjust connects proposed changes to the physical layout of a site. The design challenge was to help users review, understand, and refine a change before it becomes operational.

The design challenge was to make a complex system feel understandable at the moment a real decision had to be made.
Double Diamond · Discover

Start with the work, not the interface.

I treated the map as the primary reasoning surface and explored how users compare current state, proposed state, and downstream consequence.

Context

Spatial context reduces abstract risk.

Showing the change in place helps users reason about consequence.

Research lens

Review is part of the decision.

Observation, stakeholder alignment, and prototype feedback kept the work grounded in real constraints.

Double Diamond · Define

Turn complexity into a usable point of view.

We defined a workflow that moves from recommendation to comparison to intentional adjustment, with the user always in control.

01 · Discover

Understand people, constraints, hardware, data, and context.

02 · Define

Make the problem and decision model legible to the team.

03 · Develop

Prototype flows, states, and edge cases across surfaces.

04 · Deliver

Test, refine, and create reusable patterns that last.

Double Diamond · Develop

Let the map carry the explanation.

The interface makes proposed changes visible and reviewable without burying the user in configuration panels.

A spatial view turns a recommendation into something people can inspect and discuss.

Double Diamond · Deliver

Decision support with agency.

The experience supports automation while keeping professional judgement at the centre.

What changed

More confident review of change.

Users can understand why a change matters before choosing what to do.

What scales

Patterns over one-off screens.

Reusable interaction and content structures create leverage across the wider product ecosystem.
